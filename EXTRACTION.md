# Extraction scope

Origin: the development harness created at **Seraph Industries**.
Source baseline: version 2.5.3.
Previous core baseline: the generalized extraction through v2.3.5.

The source was read to identify reusable behavior. Its implementation, operational
workspaces, private reports, local paths and organization-specific policy are not
distributed here. Source version and core version are independent.

## Coverage of the intervening changes

- **Shared identifiers and traceability:** generators and validators share a grammar;
  criterion mentions do not prove execution. Doctrine 04 and 08; verification record.
- **Adoption, no-op updates and catalog cleanup:** preserve unrelated work, report no-op
  success, retain recorded human consent for cleanup. Doctrine 07 and 08; BOOTSTRAP.
- **Instruction loading and control ownership:** one root policy, explicit adapter
  coverage, protected controls and deliverable separation. Doctrine 05 and 08.
- **Independent checks and honest failures:** continue independent checks, identify
  missing prerequisites, validate structured results and exception dates. Doctrine 04
  and 08; verification record.
- **Role dispatch and availability:** capability policy, bounded delegation, observed
  execution, run-specific degradation and human disposition. Doctrine 05 and 08.
- **Pack distribution and migration:** baseline versus local ownership, provenance,
  override visibility, recipient validation and partial-update reporting. Doctrine 08;
  adoption record.
- **Guard hardening and conversational boundaries:** action versus prose, actual
  destinations, observation before blocking, no offers to bypass. Doctrine 08; AGENTS.
- **Integration audit and release integrity:** test actual host invocation, retain
  regression evidence, isolate reports, prevent approval reuse and align release
  metadata. Doctrine 08; reference adapter guide.

## Intentional generalization

The core retains PAUSED for an honest mid-unit session close. Unresolved output checks
or degraded reviews block unit completion and publication readiness, not preservation
of a truthful checkpoint. This differs from source adapters that block session exit.

No provider ladder, pricing, benchmark claim, model ID, fixed shell grammar or
software-only scanner is made universal. Research, content and operations keep their
own contracts. Role policy is configurable; a fallback does not authorize evading a
provider's safety restrictions.

The source's live pilots are evidence about its named implementations and versions.
They do not validate this documentation as executable software. This repository ships
no automatic updater, enforcement engine or runtime hook installer. Consumers must
record and validate their chosen implementation; manual use is explicitly supported.

Deterministic checks establish their defined properties only. A cited source can still
be wrong; a well-formed approval record does not authenticate its author. Substantive
judgment and authority remain human responsibilities.
